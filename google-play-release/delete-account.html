Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Fastest)

Erase all data immediately from within the application

If you have the Application installed on your device, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Burmese Money Manager application.
  2. Tap on Settings (⚙️) from the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account & Security section.
  4. Tap on Delete Account & All Data.
  5. Confirm the prompt. All stored transactions, account balances, and authentication keys will be immediately purged from your device.
