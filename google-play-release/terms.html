2. Description of the Service

Burmese Money Manager is an offline-first, client-side personal financial bookkeeping and expense tracking utility software designed to assist individuals in recording, organizing, and analyzing their personal household budgets, daily expenses, income streams, and personal savings targets in Myanmar Kyat (MMK) and global currencies.

The Service provides graphical analytics, date filtering (spanning historical to multi-year horizons), multi-account ledger segregation, and on-demand local data export (JSON and Excel .xlsx formats).
