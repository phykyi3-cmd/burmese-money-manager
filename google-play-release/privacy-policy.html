Privacy Policy & Data Safety Declaration

Burmese Money Manager ("BMM", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. We operate on a strict Offline-First & Client-Side Local Storage Architecture. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal financial transactions to any third-party or remote servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Burmese Money Manager handles user information across all supported platforms (including Android Application and Progressive Web App). This document is formulated to satisfy all requirements under the Google Play Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy and Data Safety Section guidelines.

By installing, downloading, or using Burmese Money Manager, you acknowledge the data handling practices described in this policy.

2. Strict Offline-First Architecture

Unlike conventional cloud-tethered financial applications, Burmese Money Manager functions fundamentally as a local on-device utility:

  • No Remote Transaction Databases: All income entries, expense categories, notes, account balances, and savings goals are stored directly inside your device's sandboxed storage (HTML5 LocalStorage and IndexedDB).
  • Zero Server Logging: We do not operate remote application servers that collect, monitor, or aggregate user financial behavior.
  • Full Offline Functionality: The Application works seamlessly without an active internet connection.

3. Information We Handle (Data Safety Declaration)

A. Financial & Transaction Records (Stored Locally Only)

User-entered amounts, income/expense logs, account names (e.g. KBZPay, WavePay, AYA Bank, CB Bank, Cash), dates, and descriptions. This data remains exclusively on the local client device and is never uploaded to any cloud server.

B. Authentication & User Profile (Optional)

If the user opts to sign in via Google Sign-In, the Application receives standard basic profile information (display name, email address, profile avatar) directly from Google's client SDK solely to customize the user interface on the device. Passwords and sensitive credentials are never accessed or stored.

C. Device & Preferences Information

User preferences such as preferred language (Burmese or English), primary currency symbol (MMK, USD, etc.), visual theme (dark/light), and custom passcode lock hashes are saved locally on the device.

4. Zero Third-Party Sharing & No Advertising

We commit unequivocally to the following privacy guarantees:

  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user information to data brokers, advertising agencies, or marketing companies.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not embed hidden tracking frameworks, Facebook SDKs, or third-party behavioral analytics trackers.
  • No In-App Advertisements: The Application is 100% ad-free; no advertising networks have access to your device.

5. Device Permissions Notice

Burmese Money Manager requests minimal, strictly necessary device permissions:

  • INTERNET: Used solely by the web view shell to load application assets and verify PWA service workers.
  • STORAGE / DOWNLOADS (User Initiated): When you explicitly request an Excel (.xlsx) or JSON export, standard file saving APIs allow saving the file to your device's Downloads directory.
  • NO INVASIVE PERMISSIONS: We do not request access to Camera, Contacts, SMS, Call Logs, Microphone, or Precise Location.

6. Data Retention & User Account Deletion Rights

Under Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, you have an unqualified right to delete your account, credentials, and all recorded financial entries at any time:

1. Instant In-App Erasure: Open the App > go to Settings > Account & Security > "Delete Account & All Data". Confirming this action immediately purges all LocalStorage keys, security locks, and transactions from the device.

2. Dedicated Web Deletion Portal: If you have uninstalled the app or wish to submit an erasure request, visit our dedicated portal at https://burmese-money-manager.surge.sh/account-deletion.html.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Burmese Money Manager is intended for a general audience (specifically individuals of age 18 and older managing personal finances). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately for prompt erasure.

8. Google Play Policy Alignment

This Privacy Policy is maintained to remain in strict alignment with Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Data Safety Section Accuracy: We declare No Data Shared with third parties and No Data Collected on remote servers.
  • Encryption: Sensitive local records utilize client-side protection.
  • Public Availability: This policy is permanently published at an active, non-geoblocked, publicly accessible URL.
