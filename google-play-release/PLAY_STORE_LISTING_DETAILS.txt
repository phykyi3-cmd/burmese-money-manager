[3. FULL DESCRIPTION (Max 4000 characters)]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Burmese Money Manager is an intelligent, offline-first personal finance and expense tracking suite designed to help individuals effortlessly organize, monitor, and master their daily finances with complete privacy.

Built specifically for seamless personal bookkeeping, the app requires zero internet connection, keeping 100% of your financial transaction records strictly secured inside your local device with zero cloud leaks.

KEY FEATURES:

• FAST & INTUITIVE EXPENSE LOGGING
Record daily income, expenses, and account transfers in seconds with clean categories and smart transaction notes.

• MULTI-ACCOUNT & LOCAL WALLET MANAGEMENT
Segregate and monitor balances across multiple accounts (KBZPay, WavePay, AYA Bank, CB Bank, Yoma Bank, and Cash).

• MULTI-YEAR HISTORICAL LEDGER (2015 - 2050)
Navigate your complete financial history across days, months, and decades. Seamlessly review past transactions or plan upcoming budget milestones.

• SMART SAVINGS GOALS TRACKER
Set custom savings targets with visual milestone progress bars, deadlines, and automated progress calculations.

• VISUAL ANALYTICS & INSIGHTFUL REPORTS
Gain deep clarity on your spending habits with interactive breakdown charts, income-to-expense ratios, and category summaries.

• 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE ARCHITECTURE
Your financial records belong exclusively to you. No remote tracking, no server telemetry, and no third-party data collection.

• FLEXIBLE DATA BACKUP & EXPORT
Export your entire ledger into Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or raw JSON backup files at any time with a single tap.

• CLIENT-SIDE APP LOCK SECURITY
Safeguard your financial privacy from unauthorized access with a built-in security PIN passcode lock.

• BEAUTIFUL MODERN DESIGN
Clean dark luxury design crafted for maximum legibility and comfort.
